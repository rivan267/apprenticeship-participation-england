Apprenticeships
Academic year 2025/26

Description

This document describes the contents of the underlying data files accompanying the ‘Apprenticeships’ statistics publication. There is a Methodology document (https://explore-education-statistics.service.gov.uk/methodology/further-education-and-skills-statistics-methodology) that is linked under ‘Quick links’ at the top of the release that contains further information on the statistics published here.

Coverage

England

Data files

Achievement Rates Providers - Volumes and Rates by Provider, SSA T1, Level, Standard-framework name

Filename: na05_app_narts_provider_level_fwk_std_ptype_202526_14.csv
Geographic levels: Provider
Time period: 2022/23 to 2024/25
Content summary: Apprenticeship national achievement rate tables. Updated in April 2026 to include provider type

Variable names and descriptions for this file are provided below:

Variable name        |  Variable description
-------------------  |  ----------------------------------------
achievement_rate     |  Achievement rate
achievers            |  Achievers
age_group            |  Age group
age_youth_adult      |  Age (under 19/19+)
completers           |  Completers
leavers              |  Leavers
level                |  Apprenticeship level
pass_rate            |  Pass rate
provider_type        |  Provider type
retention_rate       |  Retention rate
ssa_tier_1           |  Sector subject area (tier 1)
std_fwk_name_stcode  |  Framework / standard name (with st code)

Footnotes:

1. Achievement rates are based on the individual apprenticeship programmes that were completed in the relevant year (Hybrid End Year). They are calculated as the number of programme aims achieved divided by the number started, excluding the programme aims of any learners that transferred onto another qualification within the same institution.
2. Achievement rates are calculated according to the Apprenticeship Qualification Achievement Rate business rules. These documents are available from the Introduction to Qualification Achievement Rates (QARs) (https://www.gov.uk/guidance/introduction-to-qualification-achievement-rates-qars#end-of-use-of-minimum-standards) guidance.
3. For Apprenticeships, age is calculated based on age at start of the programme rather than based on 31 August. The following age bands have been used in this report: 16-18, 19-23, 24+.
4. Pass rates are based on the individual aims that were successfully completed in the relevant year (the Hybrid End Year). They are calculated as the number of learning aims achieved divided by the number successfully completed.
5. Percentages are rounded to one decimal place and calculated on unrounded volumes. 'low' indicates a percentage below 0.5%. Where data shows 'x' this indicates data is unavailable, 'z' indicates data is not applicable, and 'c' indicates data is suppressed.
6. Retention rates are based on the individual aims that were successfully completed in the relevant year (the Hybrid End Year). They are calculated as the number of learning aims completed divided by the number of leavers.
7. The overall achievement rate is based on the Hybrid End Year. The Hybrid End Year is the later of the Achievement Year, Expected End Year, Actual End Year or Reporting Year of a programme.

Adverts and vacancies by Level, month

Filename: apps_36_vacancies-adverts_202526_10.csv
Geographic levels: National
Time period: 2018/19 to 2025/26
Content summary: Adverts and vacancies as reported on the Find an apprenticeship website.

Variable names and descriptions for this file are provided below:

Variable name   |  Variable description
--------------  |  --------------------
adverts         |  Adverts
apps_level_faa  |  Apprenticeship level
month           |  Month
vacancies       |  Vacancies

Footnotes:

1. Adverts are vacancy notices and will include at least one apprenticeship vacancy with an employer (one advert could potentially include a large number of individual vacancies).
2. Level is as declared by the user placing the advert and does not necessarily align with the definition of level we publish elsewhere. Higher apprenticeships here include those declared as 'Higher' and 'Degree' in the advert. Degree apprenticeships here are those higher apprenticeships at level 6 or 7 apprenticeships declared as "Degree". Elsewhere we define a degree apprenticeship as a level 6 or 7 apprenticeship with a mandatory degree component that is required for successful achievement of the apprenticeship.
3. The number of adverts does not provide a true measure of adverts for apprenticeships because many are not advertised through the Find An Apprenticeship website, therefore the measure represents a subset of the total number available across the marketplace as a whole.
4. The number of vacancies could include some over-counting as it may be possible for a vacancy to have previously been advertised and some vacancies can be extended.
5. The number of vacancies does not provide a true measure of vacancies for apprenticeships because many are not advertised through the Find An Apprenticeship website, therefore the measure represents a subset of the total number available across the marketplace as a whole.


Commitments by Start year, Start month, Payment Status, Level, Age, Levy status

Filename: apps_25_app_service_commitments_202526_10.csv
Geographic levels: National
Time period: 2025/26
Content summary: Commitments recorded on the apprenticeship service as at 11 June 2026.

Variable names and descriptions for this file are provided below:

Variable name        |  Variable description
-------------------  |  --------------------
age_summary          |  Age group
apps_level           |  Apprenticeship level
commitments          |  Commitments
latest_levy_status   |  Latest levy status
payment_status       |  Payment status
start_academic_year  |  Start academic year
start_month          |  Start month
transfer_flag        |  Transfer flag

Footnotes:

1. Commitments may be recorded on the Apprenticeship Service system after the training start date has passed and therefore data should be treated as highly provisional.
2. From 01 April 2021, all non-levy paying organisations are using the apprenticeship service. Prior to this date some starts with non-levy employers were not funded through the apprenticeship service and so the data reported here only provides a partial picture.
3. Fully agreed commitments include commitments for which funding payments are complete, along with those that are actively being funded.
4. The levy-status of a commitment is based on the latest known status of the corresponding apprenticeship service account. This means that historical commitments may change their levy status if the apprenticeship service account was to subsequently change their status.
5. This data does not capture all apprenticeships as prior to 01 April 2021 not all non-levy employers were using the apprenticeship service (AS). From 01 April 2021 all new starts are reported and managed through the AS.


Duration, Starts by Level, Age, Length of employment

Filename: apps_05_duration_length_employment_202526_10.csv
Geographic levels: National
Time period: 2020/21 to 2025/26
Content summary: Apprenticeship expected duration, length of employment, age, detailed level

Variable names and descriptions for this file are provided below:

Variable name                      |  Variable description
---------------------------------  |  -----------------------------------------------
age_summary                        |  Age
apps_level_detailed                |  Detailed level
average_expected_duration          |  Average expected duration (days)
foundation_shorter_apprenticeship  |  Foundation / shorter apprenticeship flag

starts                             |  Starts
starts_expected_under_12_months    |  Starts having expected duration under 12 months

Footnotes:

1. Age for starts is based on age at the start of the programme.

3. Expected duration is based on learning start date and planned end date as recorded on the ILR. Re-starting learners who may have returned from a planned break are excluded. Learners transferring their apprenticeship to a new provider are also excluded.
4. Length of Employment is based on the First Day of Learning with the current employer as recorded in the ILR.
5. Where shown, figures for 2025/26 are provisional and cover the first three quarters (Aug to April). Figures for other years are final and cover the full year (Aug to July).
