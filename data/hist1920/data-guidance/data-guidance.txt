Find an apprenticeship adverts and vacancies - December 2020

Filename: app-vacancies-adverts-2020-dec.csv
Geographic levels: National
Time period: 2018/19 to 2020/21
Content summary: In progress

Variable names and descriptions for this file are provided below:

Variable name  |  Variable description
---------  |  ---------
Adverts    |  Adverts
Level      |  Level
Month      |  Month
Vacancies  |  Vacancies

Footnotes:

1. The 2020/21 academic year only currently contains 4 months worth of data, whereas previous years are full academic years
2. Level is as declared by the user placing the advert and does not necessarily align with the definition of level we publish elsewhere. Higher apprenticeships here include those declared as 'Higher' and 'Degree' in the advert. Degree apprenticeships here are those higher apprenticeships at level 6 or 7 apprenticeships declared as "Degree". Elsewhere we define a degree apprenticeships as a level 6 or 7 apprenticeship with a mandatory degree component that is required for successful achievement of the apprenticeship
3. A vacancy is a unique opportunity to undertake an apprenticeship with an employer and the number of vacancies provides a count of apprenticeship vacancies published on FAA in a particular period
4. The number of vacancies could include some over-counting as it may be possible for a vacancy to have previously been advertised and some vacancies can be extended
5. The number of adverts does not provide a true measure of adverts for apprenticeships because many are not advertised through the Find An Apprenticeship website, therefore the measure represents a subset of the total number available across the marketplace as a whole
6. The number of vacancies does not provide a true measure of vacancies for apprenticeships because many are not advertised through the Find An Apprenticeship website, therefore the measure represents a subset of the total number available across the marketplace as a whole
7. Figures are rounded to the nearest 10. Data that are not yet available are indicated by a z
8. Adverts are vacancy notices and will include at least one apprenticeship vacancy with an employer (one advert could potentially include a large number of individual vacancies)
